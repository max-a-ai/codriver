# Status

Check this tab first, especially after a computer restart: the GNSS comes up at
1 Hz and stays there until somebody sets it.

GNSS and the lidars are on the left, the cameras on the right. Green means the
topic is publishing at its expected rate, red means it is absent, silent, or off
rate. The fisheye and the two bumper lidars show their own red light but never
fail their box.

Nothing on this tab does anything. There is no button to press here on purpose:
this is the screen you read, not the one you act on. If a lidar is dark, go to
Monitoring for the operating-mode buttons before assuming hardware, and use the
sweep button there to re-check.
