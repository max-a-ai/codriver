# Status

Check this tab first, especially after a computer restart: the GNSS comes up at
1 Hz and stays there until somebody sets it.

Green means the topic is publishing at its expected rate, red means it is
absent, silent, or off rate. The fisheye and the two bumper lidars show their
own red light but never fail their box.

If a lidar is dark, try the operating-mode buttons at the bottom before
assuming hardware.
